# Test Results for `tor41350` & `hsdir_post_metrics` Patch Series

Baseline: C Tor `tor-0.4.9.14` (`8fe2745`)

## Patches in `upstream/tor41350/`

1. `0001-rephist-Pair-opened-connection-gauge-decrements-with.patch` (`tor#41350`)
   - Tracks `in_rephist_opened` and the counted `(rephist_from_listener, rephist_type, rephist_socket_family)` tuple on `connection_t`.
   - Calls `rep_hist_note_conn_closed()` in `connection_mark_for_close_internal_()` and `connection_free_minimal()` only when `in_rephist_opened` is set, using the stored tuple and clearing the bit.
   - Adds unit test `stats/rephist_conn_opened` in `src/test/test_stats.c` covering:
     1. Accepted `OR` and transparent `AP` connections whose `connection_init_accepted_conn()` fails before `note_connection()`;
     2. Linked `EXIT` + `DIR` (`BEGIN_DIR`) and `AP` + `DIR` (`connection_ap_make_link()`) connection pair closes;
     3. Extended ORPort (`CONN_TYPE_EXT_OR` -> `CONN_TYPE_OR`) retyping before close;
     4. Normal inbound (`from_listener=1`) and outbound (`from_listener=0`) accept/connect/close round trips;
     5. Listener connections (`listener_connection_new()`) closed normally or freed via `connection_free_minimal()` on failed `connection_add()`.

2. `0002-relay_metrics-Export-HS-descriptor-POST-outcome-coun.patch`
   - Exports `tor_relay_hsdir_post_total{status="200"|"400",reason="ok"|"rev_counter"|"bad_url"|"invalid_desc"}` on the MetricsPort.
   - Adds unit test `hs_cache/hsdir_post_outcomes` in `src/test/test_hs_cache.c` covering all four outcome buckets.
