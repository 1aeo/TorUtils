Terms
- Policy A: the exit-policy summary (consensus "p" line)
    reject 25,110,135,137-139,143,445,465,587,993,995,3389
  i.e. exit to every port except these. Every relay with A lists the
  rules in this order: 25,465,587,110,143,993,995,3389,135,137-139,445.
- Policy B: the summary
    reject 25,119,135-139,445,465,563,587,993,995,1214,
           4661-4666,6346-6429,6699,6881-6999
  i.e. tor's default exit policy plus reject 465,587,993,995.
- A and B both allow exit to port 22 (SSH).
- Affected relay: published >=1 server descriptor whose IPv4 summary
  is exactly A or B. A policy match, not proof of compromise.
- Quetzalcoatl: relays in that family's MyFamily line (362
  fingerprints) or with its ContactInfo. 277 were listed on 10-02.
- Other operators: all other relays, incl. TorDola.
- TorDola: relays TorDola and TorDola1-4 of one operator on AS211720
  (Datashield). TorDola4 is new on 10-04.
- Operator: one ContactInfo string; each of the 7 relays without one
  counts as its own operator.
- Host: one IPv4 address. Relays sharing an IPv4 count as one host;
  physical machines are not visible in the data.
- FranTech: AS53667 (FranTech Solutions, BuyVM).
- Reload: new policy, same tor start time (published minus uptime),
  i.e. config re-read. Restart: new tor start time.
- Listed: in the hourly consensus. A relay is listed when more than
  half of the 9 directory authorities (5) include it in their votes.
- Flagged: BadExit or MiddleOnly in the consensus. A flag is set when
  more than half of the authorities that use it vote for it (BadExit
  3 of 5, MiddleOnly 5 of 9). An authority that leaves a relay out
  of its vote counts as not voting for the flag.
- Usable exit: listed with Exit and without BadExit.
- Text order: IPs sorted as strings (107.189.13.x before 107.189.5.x).
- Dates MM-DD 2026, times UTC. hh:mm:ss = descriptor published, but
  tor start time in restart runs (items 4, 12). hh:00 = consensus or
  vote.

Who was affected
- 377 relays on 147 hosts, 15 ASes, 59 operators. Quetzalcoatl: 277
  relays, 73 hosts. 58 other operators: 100 relays, 74 hosts.
- On FranTech: 129/377 relays (34.2%), 82/147 hosts (55.8%), 47/59
  operators (79.7%). Other operators alone: 81/100 relays, 58/74
  hosts, 46/58 operators.
- 10-01 14:00 (last consensus before A), other operators' relays
  that later became affected: FranTech 71/101 (70.3%), all other
  ASes 13/9,054 (0.14%). Per operator: 44/59 (74.6%) of operators
  with a FranTech relay then, 10/3,604 (0.28%) of those without.
- 12 other operators affected on FranTech also had 50 relays listed
  on other ASes at 10-01 14:00: none of the 50 became affected.
- Not every affected operator used FranTech: 12 other operators (19
  affected relays, 8 ASes) had no FranTech relay 05-01..10-07.
- Quetzalcoatl: all 273 of its relays listed at 10-01 14:00 became
  affected: 48 on FranTech, 225 on its 6 other ASes (HostPapa,
  netcup, MAXKO, 3 Contabo ASes). Other operators' relays listed
  then on those 6 ASes: 0/452 became affected.
- 230/377 were flagged in >=1 consensus. 57 never got BadExit or
  MiddleOnly in any vote (10-01 00:00..10-07 01:00): 14 Quetzalcoatl
  (all B) and 43 others, incl. all 5 TorDola; 36 had B, 13 A, 8
  both. 56 of the 57 were usable exits for a time. 20 of them still
  publish (newest 10-08 05:35:35); 1-3 of 9 authorities list them,
  so none is listed.
